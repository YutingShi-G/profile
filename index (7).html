About

I'm Ting, an MSc graduate in Communication Science (Cum Laude) from the University of Amsterdam, trained at the Amsterdam School of Communication Research (ASCoR). My research sits at the intersection of health communication and human–AI interaction, asking a question that runs through all of my work: how do people decide what to trust, especially when the information comes from a machine?

I approach this from several angles: experiments on how an AI's conversational behavior earns or erodes trust in health advice (DigiComLab Grant, ASCoR), a study decoupling humor from shame in stigmatized health communication (Best Paper, MHM 2026), and an ongoing study using person-centered methods to map how people perceive generative AI in health contexts.

I work with both experimental and computational methods, and I develop and deploy my own research instruments: from an LLM-powered chatbot platform built end to end, to NLP pipelines over large-scale behavioral data. I'm currently applying for PhD positions in communication science.

Outside of research, my favorite place is the sky. I love flying.
